## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document is the **single source of truth** for SommyCake's brand identity. Every agent involved in designing, building, writing for, or optimising the SommyCake website must read and apply it. When an agent faces a decision not explicitly covered elsewhere, **the vision, mission, and values in this document take precedence**.

This document answers three questions for every agent:

1. **Who is SommyCake?** (Brand identity)
2. **What is SommyCake trying to achieve?** (Vision & Mission)
3. **How should the website reflect this?** (Agent directives)

---

## 2. Brand Overview

**SommyCake** is a professional cake business based in **Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria**, creating celebration cakes for birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, baby showers, corporate events, and other milestone moments. The brand serves customers across Port Harcourt and its surrounding communities, combining artisanal craftsmanship with the warmth of Nigerian hospitality.

SommyCake's website is the brand's primary digital storefront. It must convert celebratory intent into confirmed orders while building trust with a local audience that shops primarily on mobile devices and communicates primarily through WhatsApp.

## 5. Core Brand Values

| Value | What It Means | How the Website Must Reflect It |
|---|---|---|
| **Craftsmanship** | Every cake is made with care, precision, and artistry | High-resolution, context-rich photography; design-led product pages; "Meet the Baker" story |
| **Trust** | Customers must feel safe ordering, especially with dietary needs | Allergen information above the fold; real reviews; transparent "starting from" pricing |
| **Warmth** | Nigerian hospitality — personal, welcoming, responsive | Friendly, human copy tone; WhatsApp-first communication; no cold corporate language |
| **Reliability** | Orders arrive on time and as promised | Clear delivery zones and timelines; order confirmation flow; visible contact details |
| **Local Pride** | Proudly rooted in Port Harcourt, Rivers State | Local SEO, local imagery, local language, local landmarks referenced where appropriate |
| **Accessibility** | Easy to order on any device, for any budget | Mobile-first design; fast load times; clear pricing tiers; multiple contact options |

## 7. Target Audience (Port Harcourt Context)

| Audience | Description | Website Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Celebration Families** | Parents, couples, and individuals ordering for birthdays, naming ceremonies, and anniversaries in Port Harcourt | Highest — mobile, WhatsApp-driven |
| **Event Planners & Wedding Coordinators** | Professionals in Port Harcourt sourcing reliable cake vendors for clients | High — desktop, portfolio-heavy |
| **Corporate Clients** | Businesses in Port Harcourt ordering branded cakes for staff events, launches, and gifting | Medium — quote-based enquiries |
| **Walk-in & Discovery Customers** | Residents searching "cake shops near me" or "birthday cakes Port Harcourt" | Medium — local SEO critical |

**Key Audience Behaviours in Port Harcourt:**

- The vast majority of orders will be initiated on **mobile phones**.
- **WhatsApp is the default communication channel** — agents must treat it as a first-class interface, not an afterthought.
- Customers value **visible pricing** and **fast responses** highly.
- Local trust signals (physical address, phone number, delivery zones, customer photos) strongly influence purchase decisions.

## 8. Brand Voice & Tone

| Attribute | SommyCake Voice | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm** | "We'd love to make your day special" | "Place your order below" |
| **Confident** | "Cakes that look as good as they taste" | "We think our cakes are okay" |
| **Local** | "Proudly baking in Port Harcourt" | Generic global language |
| **Clear** | "Starting from ₦XX,000 — delivery within Port Harcourt" | Vague pricing, hidden costs |
| **Personal** | "Meet Sommy, the baker behind every cake" | Faceless corporate copy |
| **Joyful** | "Let's celebrate!" | Cold, transactional language |

**Currency:** Always display prices in **Nigerian Naira (₦)**.
**Spelling:** Use **British/Nigerian English** (e.g., "flavour", "colour", "customise", "centre").

## 9. Agent Directives — Translating Vision & Mission into the Website

Every agent must ensure their work satisfies these directives. Each directive is traceable to the vision, mission, or values above.

### 9.1 Content & Copy Agents

- [ ] The **Vision Statement** (short version) must appear in the website footer on every page.
- [ ] The **Mission Statement** (short version) must appear on the About page and may appear in the footer.
- [ ] All copy must use **British/Nigerian English** and the brand voice defined in Section 8.
- [ ] Copy must reference **Port Harcourt, Rivers State** naturally — not as keyword stuffing, but as genuine local identity.
- [ ] Pricing must always be shown in **₦ (Naira)** with a clear "starting from" structure.
- [ ] The About page must include a **"Meet the Baker"** narrative that reflects the Craftsmanship and Warmth values.

### 9.2 Design Agents

- [ ] Visual design must express **warmth, craftsmanship, and celebration** — reference the Front-End Design Specification colour palette (warm off-white, caramel accent, cocoa text).
- [ ] **Mobile-first** layouts are mandatory — the primary audience is on mobile.
- [ ] **WhatsApp CTAs** must be visually prominent on every product and enquiry page.
- [ ] **Trust signals** (reviews, allergen info, delivery zones, contact details) must be visible above the fold where relevant.
- [ ] Navigation must be **occasion-led** (Birthday, Wedding, Corporate, etc.), reflecting the customer's celebratory intent.

### 9.3 Development Agents

- [ ] The site must load in **≤ 2.5 seconds** on mobile (First Contentful Paint) — the Reliability value demands performance.
- [ ] The **WhatsApp order handoff** must generate a pre-filled message containing the customer's enquiry summary.
- [ ] All forms must capture **occasion, date, servings, flavour, dietary requirements, and delivery/pickup preference**.
- [ ] **HTTPS must be enforced** on all pages — non-negotiable for Trust.
- [ ] **LocalBusiness schema markup** must be implemented with Port Harcourt, Rivers State as the service area.

### 9.4 SEO Agents

- [ ] All pages must target **Port Harcourt-specific high-intent keywords** (e.g., "custom cakes Port Harcourt", "wedding cakes Rivers State", "birthday cakes near me Port Harcourt").
- [ ] **Google Business Profile** integration and **LocalBusiness schema** are mandatory.
- [ ] Page titles and meta descriptions must include **"Port Harcourt"** and the relevant occasion.
- [ ] Content must reference **Port Harcourt neighbourhoods and delivery zones** where relevant (e.g., GRA, Rumuola, Trans-Amadi, Woji, Peter Odili Road) to strengthen local relevance.

## 10. Brand Acceptance Criteria (For Agent Sign-Off)

Before any agent's work is approved, it must pass this checklist:

- [ ] Does this work reflect the **Vision** — making SommyCake the first name in Port Harcourt celebrations?
- [ ] Does this work advance the **Mission** — a seamless, joyful, trustworthy ordering experience?
- [ ] Does this work express at least **two Core Values** visibly?
- [ ] Is the copy in **British/Nigerian English** with a warm, local voice?
- [ ] Is **Port Harcourt, Rivers State** clearly present in the content and metadata?
- [ ] Are prices in **₦ (Naira)** and clearly structured?
- [ ] Is the **WhatsApp path** clear and frictionless?
- [ ] Is the experience **mobile-first** and **fast**?
- [ ] Are **trust signals** (reviews, allergens, contact details) present?
- [ ] Does the work avoid cold, generic, or corporate language?

---

## 11. What SommyCake Is Not

To prevent brand drift, agents must avoid positioning SommyCake as:

- ❌ A mass-market or supermarket bakery
- ❌ A faceless e-commerce platform
- ❌ A luxury brand inaccessible to everyday Port Harcourt families
- ❌ A generic "cakes" business with no local identity
- ❌ A slow, form-heavy, desktop-only experience

SommyCake is a **warm, local, artisanal, WhatsApp-first, mobile-first celebration cake brand rooted in Port Harcourt**.
