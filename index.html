Meet the Baker

"In Every Cake, A Personal Celebration."

Welcome to SommyCake. I am Precious Okeke Chisom (Sommy), founder and head cake designer. I believe that when a family or client trusts us with their milestone, they aren't just ordering flour and sugar—they are trusting us with their memories.

Based at Mirinwanyi-Asa, Oyigbo, Rivers State, SommyCake was born out of a desire to eliminate mass-produced shortcuts. Every batch is baked fresh on the morning of delivery, using pure dairy butter, rich cocoa, and natural vanilla. We take the time to listen, customize, and ensure that what arrives at your celebration looks breathtaking and tastes unforgettable.

Help & Ordering Info

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you order your celebration cake with complete confidence.

For standard celebration cakes and bento cakes, we recommend 24 to 48 hours notice. For large multi-tier wedding cakes and custom themed sculptures, we advise booking 2 to 4 weeks ahead to ensure date reservation and bespoke styling.

Every cake is pre-chilled in our studio to firm the buttercream and fillings before departure. Multi-tier cakes are reinforced with internal food-safe wooden dowels and delivered strictly via air-conditioned vehicles with trained handlers who know how to protect delicate sugarcraft.

Yes, absolutely! A significant number of our clients order from the UK, US, Canada, and Europe to celebrate loved ones back home. You can place your enquiry on our WhatsApp form and complete payment seamlessly via verified bank transfer or international debit cards. We can even capture photos and videos of the surprise delivery for you!

Yes! We take food allergies very seriously. Please specify in our enquiry form if you need nut-free preparation, eggless recipes, or reduced-sugar sponges. We maintain strict sanitation procedures in our kitchen.

Because every cake is freshly customized with perishable ingredients, we require payment confirmation before baking commences. Standard celebration cakes require 100% upfront confirmation, while wedding cakes require a 50% deposit to lock your date and the balance before dispatch.
